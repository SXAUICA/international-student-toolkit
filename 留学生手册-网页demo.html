到校 24 小时内：临时住宿登记Within 24 hours: Residence registration

这是法律要求，不能拖Required by law — do not delay

外国人入住宿舍或租房，必须在 24 小时内 到公安机关办理住宿登记。未办理会被警告，可并处 2000 元以下罚款，并影响后续签证延期和居留许可。 Foreigners staying in a dorm or rented flat must register with the local police within 24 hours. Failure may lead to a warning, a fine up to ¥2000, and problems with your visa extension or residence permit.

怎么做What to do

  1. 告诉志愿者或老师你已经入住 — 我们会安排人带你去。Tell a volunteer or your teacher that you have moved in — we will take you there.
  2. 带护照原件 + 签证 / 入境章 + 住宿证明（宿舍证明或租房合同）。Bring your passport, visa/entry stamp and proof of address (dorm letter or rental contract).
  3. 拿到《境外人员临时住宿登记表》后 拍照留存，后面办卡、开户、办居留许可都要用。After getting the registration form, photograph it — you will need it for a SIM card, bank account and residence permit.

去哪儿Where to go

  • 住宿舍：太谷区出入境接待大厅（晋中市公安局太谷分局出入境管理大队）Living on campus: Taigu Entry-Exit Reception Hall
  • 住校外：属地派出所，以老师确认为准Living off campus: your local police station — confirm with your teacher

    生病了怎么办If you get sick

    第一次去医院，找人陪你去For your first visit, take a volunteer

    1. 在群里说，或直接打值班电话。Post in the group chat, or call the duty phone.
    2. 值班志愿者会陪你去，帮忙挂号、跟医生说病情。A volunteer will go with you, register you and explain your symptoms to the doctor.
    3. 保留所有发票和病历，报销要用。Keep every receipt and medical record — you need them for reimbursement.

    去哪家医院Which hospital

    上课与学习Classes & study

    学习通怎么用Using the Xuexitong app

    1. 下载「学习通」，用手机号注册。Install the app and register with your phone number.
    2. 扫码或输入老师给的课程码，加入课程。Scan the QR code or enter the course code from your teacher.
    3. 资料、PPT、作业都在课程里，考勤签到也在上面。Slides, homework and attendance sign-in are all inside the course.
    4. 不同老师用不同平台，开学第一节课记得问清楚。Teachers use different platforms — ask in the first class.

    这些都可以主动问老师要Things you can always ask for

    • 课程大纲（course outline）— 知道这学期要学什么A course outline — so you know the topics in advance
    • PPT 和讲义 — 最好提前给，方便你翻译Slides and handouts — ideally before class, so you can translate
    • 考试时间 — 越早知道越好Exam dates — the earlier the better

    常见问题FAQ

    护照丢了怎么办？I lost my passport
    第一步：立刻告诉志愿者和老师，并到派出所报警拿报案证明；第二步：联系你国家的驻华使领馆补办护照；第三步：拿到新护照后重新办理签证/居留许可。整个过程我们会全程陪你去。 Step 1: tell a volunteer and your teacher, and report it to the police station for a certificate. Step 2: contact your embassy to replace the passport. Step 3: re-apply for your visa/residence permit with the new passport. We will accompany you throughout.
    手机卡没信号 / 流量用完了No signal or data
    先看手机是不是开了飞行模式；再重启一次。还是不行就在群里说，我们带你去营业厅查（太谷城区可一趟办完）。 Check aeroplane mode, then restart. If it still fails, post in the group — we will take you to the China Mobile branch.
    食堂卡怎么充值？How to top up my canteen card
    食堂门口有自助充值机，也可以问志愿者带你操作一次。第一次充 50–100 元就够用一段时间。 There are self-service machines at the canteen entrance, or ask a volunteer to show you. ¥50–100 lasts a while.
    宿舍停电、没热水、要报修Power, hot water or repairs
    先找宿舍管理员；说不清楚就在群里发照片和位置，志愿者帮你翻译并联系后勤。 Start with the dorm manager. If language is a problem, send a photo and your location in the group — a volunteer will translate and contact maintenance.
    我没找到答案I still have a question
    在群里直接问，或者把问题写在下面这行——志愿者每天会看一次，常见问题会补进这一页。 Ask in the group chat. Volunteers check daily and add frequent questions to this page.
